## Abstract & Overview

The Fleetmikewood Protocol establishes a theoretical and practical framework that treats the mammalian genome as an adaptive, read-write information processing system. Rather than viewing genetic code as a static blueprint, this protocol details an architecture for "Informational Genetic Engineering"—the systematic application of structured data streams to influence real-time phenotypic responses and epigenetic signaling pathways.

The protocol interfaces cognitive-behavioral programming, AI-optimized linguistic scripts, and focused intentionality models (such as structured prayer and self-hypnosis) to serve as external informational inputs. These inputs are designed to interact with the body's biophysical mechanisms, exploring the intersection of neuroception, psychoneuroimmunology (PNI), and cellular transcription.

## Theoretical Foundations & Contextual Correlates

The foundational premises of the Fleetmikewood Protocol align with and build upon established research examining the mind-body matrix and cellular plasticity:

* **Epigenetic Modulation via Cognitive States:** Investigation into how targeted mental states, deep relaxation, and focused auto-suggestion can alter the expression of genes involved in the inflammatory response, stress regulation, and cellular longevity.
* **Harvard Hypnosis Studies:** Clinical and neuroimaging research from institutions such as Harvard University demonstrating that hypnosis induces distinct, objective changes in brain activity (e.g., altering functional connectivity in the anterior cingulate cortex and insula), confirming that targeted cognitive states can directly modulate autonomic physiology and systemic healing mechanisms.
* **Informational Biology:** The paradigm that biological structures operate via complex information networks, where linguistic, acoustic, and electromagnetic signals act as top-down regulators of bottom-up cellular processes.
